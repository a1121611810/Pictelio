# Files

- [Continue Reading & Browsing History](continue-reading-and-history.md) - The /continue domain in app-lynx — the illust browsing-history log with 30-day expiry, the novel continue-reading positions with session-end semantics, completedAt soft-delete and series-completion cascade, the two Pinia stores that own the data, the shared row component with its row-head removal action, and the Shelf segment-3 / /continue consumption surfaces.
- [Downloads, Gallery Save & Export](downloads-and-export.md) - The download subsystem end to end — the JS task model, state machine and generation guard in downloadQueueCore/downloadManager, the persisted queue and its red lines, the PictelioDownloader/GallerySaver/ShareHelper native executors with the six ugoira encoders, the JS-owned single-source file naming and author directories, and the /downloads page with its files-vs-records delete split.
- [Feed & Browsing](feed-and-browsing.md) - The app-lynx discovery and browsing domain — the four root destinations and the list surfaces hanging off them, the shared createMixFeed pagination deep module, the single-card discover carousel with its page-level scrim, global search with search-core filters, ranking, the watch-later / watchlist / mute-tag / shelf collection surfaces, related-works injection and tag neighbors, plus account-scoped content control (R18/R18G masks, AI three-state, tag mute).
- [Notifications & Delivery Probe](notifications-and-delivery-probe.md) - The pull-based in-app notification center (list, group expansion, locally derived unread badge, HTML stripping, pixiv:// target routing) plus the delivery probe built on it — a quiet-period-after-foreground poll that posts one aggregated local Android notification and counts sends and clicks on-device only, with deliberately narrow interpretation rules.
- [Novel Reader](novel-reader.md) - The app-lynx novel reading stack end to end — the novel list, the optional intro page and the three-segment list → intro → body navigation, the virtualized body page with its scroll signals, watchlist prompt and continue-reading position, long-press text selection with a self-drawn action menu, the from-scratch BYOK AI translation stack (native bridge, per-stream frame keying, chunked streaming with whole-batch fallback, cache, probe/credential verification, R18 consent), and 9-format novel export.
- [Settings, Persistence & WebDAV Backup](settings-and-backup.md) - The app-lynx settings and persistence surface — the settingsStore key registry split into device-scoped and account-scoped keys, the PrefsStorage seam over Keystore-backed secure storage, the SharedPreferences "CapacitorStorage" file and the IndexedDB dev store, credential isolation, and the WebDAV backup/restore pipeline (snapshot envelope v1, Java-side PICTELIO-ENC1 encryption, content-length verification with bounded retries, 10-snapshot rotation, merge-by-keys restore with a pre-restore snapshot).
